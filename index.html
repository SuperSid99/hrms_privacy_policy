Privacy Policy

HRMS app by Dinnisha Solution · Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information the HRMS mobile app ("the app") collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled. The app is an employee self-service tool. It is provided to employees by their employer ("your organisation"), and you can sign in only with an account your organisation has created for you.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile details: your name, employee ID, contact details, department, reporting manager and profile photo, as set up by your organisation.
  • Attendance selfies: a photo taken with the in-app camera when you mark attendance. If you mark attendance for a team member, a photo of that person is taken.
  • Location when marking attendance: your GPS location at the moment you check in or out, used to confirm you are at an approved work location.
  • Location during trips (background): when you start a business trip in the app and agree to the disclosure shown, the app records your route, including while the app is closed or not in use, so that travel expense claims can be checked against the distance actually travelled. A "Trip in progress" notification is shown the whole time. Tracking stops automatically when you complete the trip. No location is collected in the background at any other time.
  • Device identifier: an identifier for your device, used to link your account to one phone for security.
  • HR records: leave requests, attendance corrections, travel and expense claims with the bills you attach, goals and performance (KRA) entries, and messages and notifications inside the app.
  • Payroll documents: salary slips and HR documents your organisation publishes to you. These are shown in the app and are not stored on your device unless you choose to save or share them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: attendance selfies, team attendance photos and profile photos.
  • Location (while in use and "all the time"): attendance verification, and route tracking during a trip you started. Background location is requested separately, only after the in-app disclosure.
  • Photos: only when you choose a profile photo or a bill to attach. Only the photo you pick is used.
  • Notifications: to show the trip-tracking notification and HR alerts.

The app does not record audio. You can turn any permission off in your phone's settings; the features that depend on it will stop working.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide HR functions for your organisation: recording attendance, processing leave, approving and verifying expense claims, delivering payslips and documents, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Sharing

  • Your organisation: your managers, HR and authorised administrators can see the records relevant to their role, for example a manager can see a team member's attendance and trip routes when approving claims.
  • Service providers: map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, and your phone's built-in geocoding service may be used to turn coordinates into a place name. These requests contain map coordinates but not your name or account details.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by law.

Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to servers operated for your organisation. Trip points may be kept briefly on your device while you are offline and are uploaded once a connection is available. Access to records is limited by role.

Retention

Records are kept for as long as your organisation needs them for employment, payroll and legal purposes, and then deleted according to your organisation's retention rules.

Your choices and rights

You can view most of your own records in the app. To request a copy, correction or deletion of your data, or deletion of your account, contact your organisation's HR department or write to us at the address below. Some records may need to be kept where the law requires it.

Children

The app is intended for employees only and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
